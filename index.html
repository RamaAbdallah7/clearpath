Al Jahili Park · Independent arrival journey

Independent journey from parking to Al Jahili Park

ClearPath guides a Person of Determination from the car park to the park entrance and internal paths — with a clear route, step-by-step audio and AR cues, and hands-free control by voice or head movement, so no one has to guess or ask for help.

Built for the Khalifa University × Zayed Authority for People of Determination Inclusion Innovation Hackathon — Challenge 1 (Al Jahili Park).

What's inside

  • Journey Map — real GIS-anchored route with a confidence score per stage.
  • AR Navigate — camera view with a live arrow pointing to the next stage.
  • Hands-Free Setup — control the whole app by voice or head-tilt + blink, no touch needed.
  • Pre-Visit Story — a calm, illustrated walkthrough to prepare a child before arrival.
  • Report & Checklist — flag a barrier and export an accessibility checklist for the municipality.
